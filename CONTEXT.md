# Resume Adapter

A personal workflow for preparing a faithful master resume and adapting its
permitted content to individual job descriptions.

## Language

**Upload**:
An original PDF and its file metadata. Uploading does not confirm a master.

**Resume metadata**:
The candidate's name, professional role and ordered section headings identified
in the original resume. Missing facts remain unknown rather than invented.

**Converted resume**:
An HTML representation of the original PDF. Visual fidelity alone does not establish
semantic sections or correct reflow after text changes.

**Adaptable section**:
A resume section designated as eligible for tailoring to an opportunity.
Detailed permitted changes remain governed by the content preservation policy.

**Master**:
A confirmed resume version used as the source of truth for new adaptations.

**Adaptation**:
A proposed tailoring of permitted master content to a job description.

**Application**:
A finalized adaptation referencing the exact master version from which it was created.
